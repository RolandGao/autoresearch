One seed per cell, so a pick within about 0.15 pp of another candidate is a coin flip (single-run SD 0.12 pp; Experiment 6). The conv cells are informative: across candidates spanning x7.7 the truth varies by 0.21-2.55 pp (mean 1.04). The head and bias cells mostly are not: their truth varies by only 0.07-0.56 pp (mean 0.21-0.26), so whichever candidate a lookahead picks costs about as much as one run's noise (mean truth lost 0.06-0.13 pp).

Conv: which cooldown is used matters much less than where the decision is made. In 16 of the 24 cells all five lookaheads pick the same candidate. They match the truth's pick in 8-11 of 24 cells and lose 0.077-0.105 pp of truth on average; the tied cooldown (s_tied) is best by a small margin (11 hits, 0.077 pp). On average the picks lie 0.17-0.29 grid steps below the truth's best, a much weaker small-LR bias than Experiment 12 found on the constant plan.

The bias is concentrated in one place: without a transform, intervals 2-4. In interval 2 every lookahead picks 0.078 against the truth's 0.13 (0.26 pp lost); in interval 3 all but lin_main pick 0.047 against 0.078; in interval 4 only s_tied picks the truth's 0.078. Under the ramp, the reverse ramp and the steeper ramp, intervals 2-4 are mostly picked right, where the truth is steep (up to 2.6 pp across the candidates). In interval 1 the error has no consistent sign: picks are one grid step too small under the ramp, the steeper ramp and the wave (linear cooldowns), and one step too large without a transform and under the inverted wave.

The searched cooldown multipliers are not concentrated at 0.22. Over all transforms and candidates, 0.22 is chosen for 16-28% of the searched multipliers, with the rest spread over 0.047-1 (conv's own m: 0.13 29, 0.078 27, 0.22 24, 0.36 22 and 0.6 8 of 120). The exception is the tied multiplier of the other three LRs without a transform when head or a bias is the main LR: 0.22 in 16, 19 and 17 of 20 candidates. Over all transforms it is 0.22 in 23-28% of candidates.
