The rest-of-run linear lookahead decides like the truth: its 8-seed pick is the truth's best in intervals 2-4 (interval 5 has no lookahead), and in interval 1 it picks 0.13 where the truth ties 0.13 and 0.22 (93.83 both). One seed's pick by it loses 0.001-0.050 pp of final TTA per interval, about what one seed's pick by the truth itself loses (0.009-0.039): the truth is flat near its optimum, so a neighbouring pick costs little.

One-interval (40-step) lookaheads are biased toward small LRs once training is under way: in interval 2 every one of them picks 0.047-0.078 where the truth peaks at 0.13 (regret 0.08-0.29 pp per seed), and c40_0.6 picks 1-2 grid steps below the truth's best in intervals 2-4. The mildest cooldown, c40_0.22, is the least biased (regret 0.03-0.08 pp from interval 2 on) but still loses to rest_lin in intervals 2 and 3.

Scoring candidates from the same trained state does not cancel the seed noise: one seed's difference between two candidates' truths has an SD of 0.12-0.27 pp in intervals 1-2, as large as two independent full runs (0.17), falling to 0.06-0.17 pp in intervals 4-5 as fewer steps remain. Differences of 0.05-0.1 pp between neighbouring candidates are therefore not resolved by one seed, but they also cost little.

Cost at BS 2000: rest_lin costs the rest of the run per candidate (200, 160, 120, 80, 40 steps for intervals 1-5), so about 3 candidates per interval cost 1800 steps = 9 x a run for one searched LR, within the campaign's 20x budget.

At BS 500 (8 seeds) and BS 125 (4 seeds) the picture is the same, with flatter optima. The truth's top two or three candidates in each interval are within about 0.05 pp of each other, so one seed's pick by any good score costs little (rest_lin: 0.006-0.059 pp per interval at BS 500, 0.000-0.051 at BS 125). The short cooldowns again pick low where training is under way: at BS 125, interval 2, every one of them picks 0.017 where the truth peaks at 0.047 (regret 0.13-0.17 pp), and in interval 3 they pick 0.01 for the truth's 0.017; at BS 500 the 0.22 x cooldown is one step low in interval 2 only (0.047 for 0.078). Over all three batch sizes, one seed's decision costs on average 0.020 pp with rest_lin and 0.036 pp with c40_0.22; picking the largest candidate within 0.1-0.3 pp of the best instead does not reduce either (noise, not bias, dominates single decisions).
