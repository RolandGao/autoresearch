Five constant conv intervals on the x0.6 grid can match the baseline under every transform: each search converged within 0.25 pp of the seed-0 baseline (93.92). By transform: none 94.09, ramp 93.88, reverse ramp 94.17, wave 93.93, inverted wave 94.03, ramp x1/8 -> x8 93.84. Each is the best of 414-744 single runs on one seed, so the small gains above the baseline are partly selection on noise (SD 0.12 pp per run); the two shortfalls (-0.04, -0.08 pp) are within noise.

The searches move far from where they start. The grid-snapped oracle (the 5 interval means of baseline / transform) scores 93.07-93.73. In three of the six transforms (none, ramp, wave) that is below doing nothing (the baseline's specified schedule under the transform). Converging took 1-3 moves (414-744 full runs, 0.8-1.5 x 10^5 training steps).

The converged schedules leave the oracle's shape where it is costly. Without a transform the winner starts low and peaks in interval 2 (0.078, 0.13, 0.078, 0.047, 0.01), unlike the linear decay from 0.22. This agrees with Experiment 32's truth, whose best conv candidate in interval 1 is 0.078. Under the ramp and the inverted wave the winners hold 0.36 for the first two intervals; under the steeper ramp the first interval ends at 1.0.
