v29 needs its whole budget, and the transformed cells fail first when it is cut. At 15 full runs (v37) conv only is 0.099 pp short of the baseline on average (7 of 12 cells within 0.1 pp; BS 125 under the ramp 93.52, BS 500 under the ramp 93.70), against 0.023 pp and 12 of 12 at 20 runs; with 4 LRs, where the level scan takes about 4 runs first, the ramp cells collapse (BS 2000 79.90, BS 500 89.30, BS 125 90.90). At 10 runs (v36) the searches fail almost everywhere (conv only 3.3 pp short on average, 4 LRs 18 pp, some runs diverged).

The failure is in the first pass: when a search runs out of budget there, each remaining interval is trained at the value its climb starts from, the previous interval's, with no evaluation. Under the ramp the schedule must fall by about x0.4 per fifth of the run, so held values are several grid steps too high late in the run. A budget below 20 runs would need a different fallback (for example continuing the previous intervals' trend), not just fewer decisions.


Starting each first-pass climb on the trend removes the collapse: at 15 runs v39's 4-LR ramp cells score 93.84 / 94.02 / 94.09 at BS 2000 / 500 / 125 (v37: 79.90 / 89.30 / 90.90), and v39 is 0.091 pp short conv only (9 of 12 within 0.1 pp) and 0.108 pp with 4 LRs (7 of 11); the trend overshoots where the schedule turns, under the wave (BS 2000 with 4 LRs 92.97).
