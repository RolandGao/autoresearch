The conv search works with 4 LRs; the other groups' levels do not. Each search's conv schedule with head and the biases on the baseline scores 93.83 and 93.76, as good as the conv-only searches, while the full searched schedules score 93.67 and 93.37: the searched levels of head, norm_bias and whiten_bias cost 0.16 and 0.39 pp.

The cost is spread over the groups and depends on the level each landed on. In the worse search (whiten_bias 3500), putting whiten_bias back on the baseline recovers 0.22 pp and lowering it one x7.7 step recovers 0.24 pp; head recovers 0.14 pp. In the better search (whiten_bias 460) no single group accounts for more than 0.08 pp, and its tied whiten_bias profile beats the baseline's (93.67 vs 93.59 with it restored). Tying the groups to conv's profile is therefore not the problem; the levels chosen in the first interval are.

A short horizon cannot rank these levels (Experiment 12-style probes, research/level_horizon.py: head, norm_bias and whiten_bias are flat at short horizons below a blow-up edge, while over a full run whiten_bias loses 0.5 pp at 3500 at BS 125 and head loses 0.3-1 pp well below its optimum), and a single-seed rest-of-run comparison of one level step (ICS v13's relevel) is too noisy to fix them: it rejected lowering whiten_bias from 3500 to 460 in the search where that gains 0.24 pp.
